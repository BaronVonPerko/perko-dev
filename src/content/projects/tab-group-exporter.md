# Tab Group Exporter

Tab Group Exporter is a Chrome extension that helps you export your browser tab groups so you can back them up, share them, or recreate them later.

## Features

- Export Chrome tab groups from your current window
- Keep your workflow organized when moving between machines or profiles
- Lightweight and focused on a single job
