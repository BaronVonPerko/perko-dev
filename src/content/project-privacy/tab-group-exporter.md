# Privacy Policy for Tab Group Exporter

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Tab Group Exporter is a Chrome extension by Chris Perko.

## What Data We Collect

Tab Group Exporter does **not** collect, store on remote servers, or transmit personal data or browsing information to the developer or any third party.

To provide its export features, the extension may temporarily access tab and tab group information in your browser (such as tab titles, URLs, and group names) **only on your device** so it can generate an export for you.

## How Data Is Stored

- Export results are generated locally in your browser.
- The extension does not send tab, tab group, or browsing data to external servers operated by the developer.
- If the extension uses browser storage (for example, `chrome.storage`) for preferences or last-used options, that data remains in your browser profile under Chrome’s storage controls.

## How Data Is Used

Tab and tab group information is used solely to create the export you request. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

## Third-Party Services

This extension does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Data Sharing

Tab Group Exporter does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Because the extension does not maintain a remote user database, there is no server-side retention of your browsing data. You can remove any locally stored extension data by clearing the extension’s storage in Chrome or by uninstalling the extension.

## Changes to This Policy

If privacy practices for Tab Group Exporter change, this policy will be updated on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
